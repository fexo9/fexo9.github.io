Free Discount Breakeven Calculator

A discount feels like growth — until it eats your profit. A 10% discount at 40% margin needs 33.33% more sales just to break even. Check yours before you run the promo.

Why this works

Profit per unit drops from your margin to (margin − discount). To earn the same total profit you must sell more units: required increase = discount ÷ (margin − discount). If the discount is bigger than your margin, every sale loses money — no volume fixes that.
