The problem

An ad that is losing money feels hard to kill — you already spent on it, and “it might turn around.” So it keeps spending. The sunk cost is gone either way; the only question is what the next dollar earns.

How it works

  1. Enter the ad's real numbers: spend, leads, sales, and what each sale is worth.
  2. Compare against breakeven and your kill rules — the sheet shows KILL or SCALE.
  3. Size the testing budget before you launch, so a test has a defined end.
  4. Log the decision and the reason, so you stop re-litigating the same ad.
