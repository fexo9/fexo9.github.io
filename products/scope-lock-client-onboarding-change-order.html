The problem

Scope creep rarely arrives as a big new request. It arrives as “while you are at it” — one more revision, one more page, one more round. Each one is small; together they eat the whole margin on the job.

How it works

  1. Onboard with a written scope, milestones, and deposit terms the client signs before work starts.
  2. When an extra arrives, log it as a change order with its price before doing the work — the price sheet does the math.
  3. Count revisions against the agreed number, so the third “quick tweak” is a paid change, not a favor.
  4. Close the job with a change-order summary that shows exactly what was added and paid.
