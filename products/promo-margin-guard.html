The problem

Discounts feel like growth: sales go up, revenue goes up. But margin pays the bills, and a discount can quietly turn a profitable product into a loss — a 10% discount at 40% margin needs 33.33% more units just to break even.

How it works

  1. Enter your margin and the discount you are considering — the breakeven math is shown, not hidden.
  2. Stack the real fees (platform, payment, shipping) on top of the discount, so the true cost is visible.
  3. Plan the promo: required volume, expected volume, and the go / no-go line.
  4. Audit past promos to see which ones actually made money.
