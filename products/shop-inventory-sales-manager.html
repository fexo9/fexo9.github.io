The problem

Small shops run inventory in their heads: what is on the shelf, what is owed, what was sold. That works until it doesn't — a stockout, a forgotten invoice, a month-end with no idea what the shop actually earned.

How it works

  1. Record stock in the live inventory sheet: quantities update as you sell.
  2. Generate invoices from the same sheet, so the sale and the stock move together.
  3. Watch the dashboard: revenue, units, and what customers owe.
  4. Work in English or Arabic — both workbooks carry the same numbers.

Inside the tool

What’s inside

15 files: EN and AR workbooks (8 sheets each), EN guide (2,495 words), AR guide (2,198 words), and an invoice generator.

Honest limits

A workbook system, not accounting software. It tracks inventory, sales, and invoices; it does not replace a tax filing or an accountant.
