The problem

Subcontractors keep financing their own jobs: payroll comes out of your pocket while pay apps sit unpaid, retainage is held for months, and a missed lien deadline can erase your leverage entirely. A 2026 survey of 492 subcontractors (reported by Contractor Magazine) found 92% floated payroll from their own pockets, 43% waited 90+ days for final payment plus retainage, and 56% missed a critical lien deadline in the past two years.

How it works

  1. Log every pay application against its schedule of values, so you always know what was billed, what was approved, and what is still open.
  2. Track retainage held per project and per pay app, with release conditions recorded next to the money.
  3. Record your own lien deadlines per project and state, with a countdown that shows what is coming due.
  4. Run the monthly checklist so nothing slips: open pay apps, held retainage, upcoming deadlines.
