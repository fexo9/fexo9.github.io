The problem

A guest damages your place, you file a claim, and the platform asks for evidence you never collected: dated photos, a timeline, an itemized cost. Without a clean evidence file, strong claims get reduced or denied.

How it works

  1. Document damage the moment you find it: dated photos, a written log, and a timeline that ties each item to the stay.
  2. Price the claim item by item with receipts or replacement quotes, so the total is checkable, not a round guess.
  3. Assemble the packet in the order a reviewer reads it: summary, timeline, photos, costs, messages.
  4. Send the claim letter template with the packet attached, then track the response.
