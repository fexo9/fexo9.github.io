The problem

An unpaid invoice is awkward to chase. Wait too long and it goes cold; chase too hard and you lose the client. Most small businesses either avoid the follow-up or send the same “just checking in” note five times.

How it works

  1. Log every invoice with its due date, amount, and chase stage, so nothing goes quiet by accident.
  2. Follow a 5-stage ladder: polite reminder, firm follow-up, final notice, then escalation — each stage has ready-to-send templates.
  3. Score each overdue invoice for risk, so you spend energy where the money is.
  4. See the whole picture on the dashboard: outstanding, overdue, and what to chase today.
