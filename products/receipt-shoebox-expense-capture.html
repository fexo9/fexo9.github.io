The problem

Receipts pile up in a shoebox, a drawer, or a camera roll, and every quarter you sit down and retype them all from scratch. That session is slow, easy to get wrong, and the reason expenses get reported late — or never.

How it works

  1. Log each receipt the day it arrives: date, vendor, category, amount, tax, payment method, and the photo's file name — about 2 minutes.
  2. Keep the photo in one folder per year.
  3. At quarter end, read the quarterly summary — totals are already computed. A 15-minute quarter-end routine replaces an afternoon of retyping.
  4. Hand the summary to your accountant — or keep it for your own records.
