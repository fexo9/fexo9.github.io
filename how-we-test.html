How We Test Every Product

We sell no illusions, no incomplete products, no cheating. Every product on this site passed the same independent file audit before it was listed. No fake reviews, no invented sales, no guarantees — the work speaks.

The 4-step audit

  1. Extract the real file. We open the actual ZIP a buyer receives — never trust a filename or a size.
  2. Recalculate everything. Every workbook is recalculated; every formula is recomputed independently from its inputs. Zero error cells, or the product does not ship.
  3. Recompute the references. Any number in a listing is recomputed from the product's own inputs — never carried forward from an old draft.
  4. Grep the claims. The whole kit is searched for guarantee language, fake proof, unverified statistics, and prototype self-descriptions. Anything found is removed or the product stays off the page.

Real numbers from real audits

  • Dead-Stock Cash Recovery: 36/36 oracle checks, 12/12 adversarial cases.
  • Scope-Lock: 0 error cells across the 7-sheet workbook.
  • STR Host Damage-Claim Evidence Packet: 17 files, quick-start checked word by word.
  • Subcontractor Pay App & Retainage: 0 error cells across all 12 workbooks, math parity verified.

What we refused to sell

These products exist, but they did not pass — so they are not on this page:

  • AI Study OS — its prompt instructed AI to draft coursework sections. We will not sell a product that helps students submit work that is not theirs. Its fix was cancelled; it stays off this page.
  • E-commerce Compliance Screening Workflow — the files call it a different name and describe themselves as an experimental prototype with uncalibrated weights. Until that is resolved honestly, it stays off.
  • QuoteGuard — file audit passed, but it is inside a 14-day protected sales test. It joins this page only after the test verdict.
  • 7 catalog products with no local file to audit — live on the marketplace, but with no deliverable we can extract and verify, we cannot stand behind them here. They stay off until their files are recovered and audited.
The rule: a product earns its place here by passing the audit — not by existing, not by being ours.
